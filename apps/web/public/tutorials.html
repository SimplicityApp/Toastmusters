Time your first speech

Open the app in your meeting, pick a speaker and press Start. Green, yellow and red come up on their own, on your video, for everyone to see.

Next: Three ways to show the timer →

Transcript

You've just been given the Timer role. Here's the whole job, in about a minute. Open Apps in your Zoom meeting, and pick the timer. You add it once, and it's there in every meeting after that. Choose the speaker and the speech type. Your club's green, yellow and red times load by themselves. Press Start when the speaker begins. The timer counts up, and your video background follows it. So the speaker can see it, and so can everyone else. Green. Yellow. Red. Press Finish when they're done. The time is saved, and the next speaker is ready. Toastmasters Timer. Tutorial 2 of 5 · 0:43

Three ways to show the timer

Timer + Camera, Timer Only or Full-Screen Timer: pick the one that suits your club, and switch any time.

Next: Load your agenda in 30 seconds →

Transcript

The timer can show up three ways. Open the mode menu at the top of the Live tab, and pick the one that suits your club. Timer plus Camera keeps your camera on, and puts the colour behind you. You can still nod, and give hand signals. Timer Only turns your video tile into one big colour card. It's the classic look, where speakers watch your tile. Full-Screen Timer fills the panel with colour. Share it, so everybody sees the same signal. Or pop it out, and park it on a second screen. Switch any time, even in the middle of a meeting. The app remembers your choice for next time. Toastmasters Timer. Free in the Zoom App Marketplace.

Tutorial 3 of 5 · 0:30

Load your agenda in 30 seconds

Paste your meeting roles or the whole EasySpeak page, drag speakers into order, and pick them from the list when it is their turn.

Next: Run Table Topics →

Transcript

Typing every speaker, role and time limit takes minutes. Skip it. Open the Agenda tab, tap Import Text, and paste your meeting roles. Or the whole EasySpeak page. Every speaker comes in with the right role, and the right time limits. Drag them into running order. Back on the Live tab, tap the name field. Everyone on your agenda is right there. Pick one, and their time limits load. Toastmasters Timer. Free in the Zoom App Marketplace.

Tutorial 4 of 5 · 0:32

Run Table Topics

Get a set of three questions, copy them to the chat, and time each answer with the right rules already set.

Next: Share the timing report →

Transcript

Table Topics is the part of the meeting where nobody knows what's coming. Here's how to run it. The generator gives you a set of three questions. Tap New set for another round. Pick a category, and the questions follow. There are twenty. Copy all puts the three questions in your clipboard, ready for the meeting chat. Time this opens the timer with the Table Topics rules already set. Green at one minute. Yellow at a minute and a half. Red at two. Toastmasters Timer. Free in the Zoom App Marketplace.

Tutorial 5 of 5 · 0:22

Share the timing report

Every speaker with their time and whether they stayed inside the limits, ready to copy into the meeting chat.

Next: Watch the product demo →

Transcript

At the end of the meeting, everyone wants to know. Who made their time? Open the Report tab. Every speaker is listed with their time, and whether they finished inside the limits. Tap Copy Report to Clipboard. The report opens as text. Copy it, and paste it into the meeting chat. Toastmasters Timer. Free in the Zoom App Marketplace.
