What is the Timer Role in Toastmasters?

Every Toastmasters meeting assigns a Timer, and it is one of the first roles most new members take on. You track how long each speaker talks, show them a green, yellow, or red signal as they pass each threshold, and report the exact times at the end. It sounds small, but it is what keeps a meeting on schedule and gives every speaker a fair share of the floor.

Standard Toastmasters Timing Rules

Each speech type has its own time range. The timer shows green when the minimum time is reached, yellow at the midpoint, and red at the maximum. In contests, finishing more than 30 seconds under the minimum or over the maximum disqualifies a speaker (Table Topics: under 1:00 or over 2:30), and clubs set their own rule for meeting awards.

  • Standard Speech (5–7 min)🟢 5:00   🟡 6:00   🔴 7:00
  • Table Topics (1–2 min)🟢 1:00   🟡 1:30   🔴 2:00
  • Evaluation (2–3 min)🟢 2:00   🟡 2:30   🔴 3:00
  • Longer Speech (7–9 min)🟢 7:00   🟡 8:00   🔴 9:00

Toastmusters Timer is pre-loaded with these rules so you can start timing immediately — no manual setup required.

How to Use the Timer in Your Meeting

You do not need an account, a download, or any setup. Open the timer, pick the speech type, and press start when the speaker begins.

  1. Open the timer. Use it in any browser at the web app, or add it to Zoom so it runs beside your meeting.
  2. Load the agenda. Paste your meeting roles and the agenda builds itself, or add speakers one at a time.
  3. Pick the speech type. Standard Speech, Table Topics, Evaluation and the other roles each carry their own green, yellow, and red thresholds already.
  4. Start when the speaker starts. The screen turns green at the minimum, yellow at the midpoint, and red at the maximum — no cards to hold up and no stopwatch to read.
  5. Read your report. Every speaker's exact time is recorded, so the Timer's report at the end of the meeting is already written for you.

Because the whole screen changes color, the speaker sees the signal from across the room without looking for a small card.

Browser Timer or Zoom App?

Both are free and both use the same timing rules. The difference is only how the speaker sees the signal.

  • In-person meetings — use the browser. Put a laptop or tablet where the speaker can see it. The full page turns green, yellow, then red, which replaces a physical set of timing cards.
  • Online meetings — use the Zoom app. Your virtual background changes color automatically, so the speaker gets the signal without anyone screen sharing or holding a card to a webcam.
  • Hybrid meetings — use both. Run the Zoom app for remote speakers and keep the browser timer visible in the room.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Timer Role in Toastmasters?

The Timer is one of the most important meeting roles in Toastmasters. The Timer tracks how long each speaker talks and signals them using colored lights — green, yellow, and red — so they stay within their allotted time. Keeping speeches on time ensures the meeting runs smoothly and every speaker gets a fair chance to practice.

What are the standard Toastmasters timing rules?

Each speech type has its own time range. Standard Speech (5–7 min): green at 5:00, yellow at 6:00, red at 7:00. Table Topics (1–2 min): green at 1:00, yellow at 1:30, red at 2:00. Evaluation (2–3 min): green at 2:00, yellow at 2:30, red at 3:00. Longer Speech (7–9 min): green at 7:00, yellow at 8:00, red at 9:00.

How does the Toastmasters timer app work in Zoom?

Toastmusters Timer integrates with Zoom as a Zoom app. When timing speeches in a Zoom meeting, the app automatically changes your virtual background color to green, yellow, or red to signal the speaker — no physical cards or screen sharing needed.

Is the Toastmasters timer free?

Yes, Toastmusters Timer is completely free to use. No account or sign-up is needed — just open it in any modern browser and start timing speeches immediately. The Zoom app integration is also free.

Can I use a timer for online Toastmasters meetings?

Yes. Toastmusters Timer has a dedicated Zoom app that automatically changes your virtual background color to green, yellow, or red as the speaker reaches each time threshold. This replaces the need for physical colored cards in virtual meetings.

What colors does the Toastmasters timer use?

Toastmasters uses a traffic-light system: green means the speaker has reached the minimum time, yellow signals the midpoint of the time range, and red indicates the speaker has reached the maximum allowed time. Speakers should wrap up before or at the red signal.

How long is a Table Topics speech in Toastmasters?

A Table Topics speech in Toastmasters is 1 to 2 minutes long. The green signal shows at 1:00 (minimum time reached), yellow at 1:30 (midpoint), and red at 2:00 (maximum time). Speakers who go over 2 minutes and 30 seconds may be disqualified from awards.

What happens if a Toastmasters speaker goes over time?

After the red signal, speakers have a 30-second grace period. If they exceed the maximum time plus 30 seconds, they may be disqualified from winning the Best Speaker, Best Table Topics, or Best Evaluator award for that meeting. Toastmusters Timer tracks the exact overage so the Timer can report precise times.
